隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

生效日期 / Effective: 2026-10-15

保存在 Mac 上的数据

任务名、任务时间、整机 CPU、内存估算、热状态、电池与供电状态，以及机型和系统版本，仅写入本机应用沙盒。应用不上传这些记录，不使用广告、遥测或分析 SDK，也不读取工作文档、键盘输入或进程名称。

资料库在启动或保存记录时清理超过 30 天的已完成任务，最多 300 条任务、50,000 个采样点。上限清理从最早完成的任务开始。损坏资料库恢复会先保留原件备份；该备份不会自动上传或自动删除。Mac 系统备份由你的系统设置管理。

购买

内购及原始应用购买由 Apple StoreKit 处理。应用通过 Apple 验证的交易和权益决定高级功能访问，不接触付款卡信息。Apple 可能依其隐私政策处理账号、交易及诊断信息。应用不把交易记录发送给开发者服务器。

你主动导出的报告

报告写入你在系统文件选择器中指定的位置。默认不包含任务名称；包含机型、系统版本、采样时间和资源指标。导出选项允许你主动加入任务名。报告的保存与后续分享由你控制；导出文件不会随资料库保留上限自动删除。

支持与联系

帮助链接打开外部 GitHub 页面。你主动提交的问题受 GitHub 的隐私政策约束；公开提交前请移除私人内容。请通过 支持问题页面联系 Julian Cooper。

English

Session titles, timing, whole-machine CPU and memory estimates, thermal state, battery and power status, hardware model, and OS version remain in the app’s local sandbox. The app does not upload this data, use advertising or analytics SDKs, inspect documents, capture keystrokes, or list process names.

On launch or when saving, the library removes completed sessions older than 30 days, and enforces limits of 300 sessions and 50,000 samples. Oldest completed sessions are removed first when limits are exceeded. Corrupt-library recovery preserves an original backup locally. System backups follow your macOS settings.

Apple handles purchases and verified entitlements through StoreKit. The app does not receive payment card details or send transactions to a developer server. Apple may process account, purchase and diagnostic information under its own policy.

Reports are saved only to the location you choose. Session titles are omitted by default and can be explicitly included; reports contain machine context, sample times and metrics. You control later sharing and deletion of exported files. Support links open GitHub, whose privacy policy applies to information you choose to submit.
